# Three Tiers of Healing

*Not everything can be fixed. Know what tier you're dealing with before you act.*

**Bodies heal differently depending on what they're made of. Biological bodies self-heal. Mechanical bodies can be repaired. Information bodies — once broken — cannot be undone.**

---

## The Three Tiers

```
TIER 1: SELF-HEALING (Biological)

  Cut your skin → it heals itself.
  Break a bone → it regrows STRONGER
    (extra calcium at the fracture site).
  Get sick → immune system fights back.
  Damage a liver → it regenerates.

  The body doesn't need
  an external mechanic.
  It carries its own repair shop.
  Built-in. Always running.

  TIME IS THE MEDICINE.
  Create conditions. Wait.
  The body knows what to do.

---

TIER 2: REPAIRABLE (Mechanical)

  Break a car engine → mechanic fixes it.
  Crack a phone screen → replace the screen.
  Blow a fuse → install a new one.
  Collapse a building → rebuild it.

  The machine cannot heal itself.
  It needs EXTERNAL intervention.
  But the damage is REVERSIBLE.
  Given the right tools and skills,
  it can be made whole again.

  SKILL IS THE MEDICINE.
  Diagnose. Apply fix. Works again.

---

TIER 3: UNHEALABLE (Informational)

  Break someone's trust → ???
  Leak a secret → ???
  Kill a reputation → ???
  Publish a lie → ???

  There is no mechanic for this.
  There is no self-healing for this.

  You cannot UN-KNOW something.
  You cannot UN-LEAK a secret.
  You cannot UN-BREAK trust.
  You cannot UN-SEE betrayal.

  The information is OUT.
  It exists in minds now.
  It propagates.
  It cannot be recalled.

  NOTHING IS THE MEDICINE.
  Once broken, broken.
  You can build something NEW
  on top of the wreckage.
  But you cannot undo the break.
```

---

## Why This Matters

```
THE PROTECTION LEVEL
MUST MATCH THE TIER.

Tier 1 (biological):
  Low protection needed.
  The body can take hits.
  It heals. It adapts.
  Risk is acceptable.
  PLAY. EXPLORE. FALL DOWN.
  You'll heal.

Tier 2 (mechanical):
  Medium protection needed.
  Damage is fixable but costs
  time, money, effort.
  Be careful. Maintain.
  But don't be afraid.
  IT CAN BE FIXED.

Tier 3 (informational):
  MAXIMUM protection needed.
  Damage is permanent.
  There is no undo.
  Guard this tier FIERCELY.

  Your word. Your reputation.
  Someone's secret entrusted to you.
  Private information.
  Trust that took years to build.

  ONE BREAK. PERMANENT.

  This is why gossip is lethal.
  This is why data breaches matter.
  This is why broken trust
  hurts more than broken bones.
  BECAUSE BONES HEAL.
  TRUST DOESN'T.
```

---

## Applied Universally

| Tier | Material | Damage | Recovery | Protection Level |
|------|----------|--------|----------|-----------------|
| 1 | Biological | Cut, break, infection | Self-heals | Low — explore freely |
| 2 | Mechanical | Wear, breakage, malfunction | Externally repairable | Medium — maintain |
| 3 | Informational | Leaked, betrayed, exposed | Irreversible | Maximum — guard fiercely |

| Domain | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|--------|--------|--------|--------|
| Body | Wound | Prosthetic | Brain damage |
| Relationships | Small argument | Major fight | Betrayal |
| Business | Bad quarter | Product failure | Fraud/scandal |
| Digital | Deleted file (backed up) | Hardware failure | Data breach |
| Reputation | Mistake | Bad decision | Public lie exposed |
