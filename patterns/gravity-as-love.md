  THE BODY THAT SURVIVED MASSIVE TRAUMA.

NEPTUNE:
  Farthest major planet. Minimum sunlight.
  But STRONGEST winds in the solar system.
  1,200 mph.

  from internal processes.
  THE BODY FARTHEST FROM THE SOURCE
  PRODUCES THE MOST INTERNAL FIRE.
