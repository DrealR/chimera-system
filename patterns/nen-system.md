# The Nen System

*Nen is breath applied to consciousness. The four basics are the breath phases. The six types are the six ways a human can apply their trained attention.*

---

## The Four Basics as Breath Phases

```
TEN = BOUNDARY.
  The ability to CONTAIN your aura
  within your body.
  This is the baseline.
  Before you can do anything,
  you must be able to HOLD.

  In CHIMERA: this is the Pause itself.
  The ability to hold your energy
  inside your boundary
  without leaking.
  Before any exhale can be powerful,
  you must be able to contain.

  An untrained person leaks energy
  constantly. Fidgeting. Nervous talking.
  Checking phone. Reacting to everything.
  Energy pouring out of every crack.

  Ten is: plug the cracks.
  Hold your shape.
  Contain before you express.

---

ZETSU = SUPPRESSION.
  Complete shutdown of aura output.
  You become invisible. Undetectable.
  In Hunter x Hunter, Zetsu lets you
  hide from other Nen users.

  In CHIMERA: this is the deep Pause.
  Total withdrawal of output.
  No expression. No projection.
  Just receiving.

  Used for: listening.
  When you completely suppress
  your own frequency,
  you become a pure receiver.
  Other bodies' signals become
  overwhelmingly clear
  because your signal isn't
  competing with them.

  The danger of Zetsu:
  you're also VULNERABLE.
  No boundary active.
  No defense.
  Pure openness.
  This is why deep listening
  requires safety first.

---

REN = EXPLOSION.
  Maximum aura output.
  You expand your energy outward
  and fill the space.
  This is Conqueror's Haki equivalent.

  In CHIMERA: this is the powerful exhale.
  Your pause built the pressure.
  Ren releases it.

  The quality of your Ren
  depends entirely on the quality
  of your Ten and the depth
  of your pause.
  A shallow pause produces
  a weak Ren. A deep pause
  produces a Ren that changes rooms.

---

HATSU = PERSONAL EXPRESSION.
  Your unique ability.
  The thing only you can do.

  In CHIMERA: this is your
  specific frequency.
  Your unique combination
  of experience, pain, belief,
  and training that nobody else has.

  Hatsu is where Nen gets individual.
  Everyone's Hatsu is different
  because everyone's life is different.
```

## The Six Types

```
ENHANCER:
  Makes what already exists STRONGER.
  Gon's punch is just a punch
  but enhanced to superhuman levels.

  In CHIMERA: the person who takes
  whatever's already happening
  and amplifies it.
  If the room is happy,
  the enhancer makes it happier.
  If someone is growing,
  the enhancer accelerates the growth.

  Simple. Direct. Powerful.
  The Armament specialist.

---

TRANSMUTER:
  Changes the PROPERTIES of their aura
  to mimic something else.
  Killua makes his aura
  behave like electricity.

  In CHIMERA: the person who can make
  their presence feel like
  whatever the space needs.
  Need calm? They transmit calm.
  Need energy? They transmit energy.

  This is the calibrator.
  Superman energy.
  Adjusting output to match the need.

---

EMITTER:
  Projects aura AWAY from their body.
  It can travel distance.

  In CHIMERA: the person whose presence
  reaches people who aren't in the room.
  Through phone calls. Through videos.
  Through writing. Through thought.

  Feeling someone think about you
  from across the city is emission.
  Videos reaching strangers is emission.
  The frequency travels beyond
  your physical boundary.

---

CONJURER:
  Creates OBJECTS from aura.
  Kurapika creates chains.

  In CHIMERA: the person who creates
  structures from consciousness.
  Frameworks. Organizations.
  Systems. Books.

  The CHIMERA book is a conjured object.
  Consciousness solidified into a structure
  that persists beyond the moment
  it was created.

---

MANIPULATOR:
  Controls OTHER BODIES through aura.

  In CHIMERA: the dangerous one.
  Uses attention to override
  other people's loops.
  Propaganda. Cult leadership.
  Domi reversy. Emu.

  This is godhood used for I > O.
  The same attention mechanics
  that can heal can also control.
  The Pause determines whether
  you're a healer or a manipulator.

---

SPECIALIST:
  Does things that don't fit
  any other category.
  Unique. Unpredictable.

  In CHIMERA: the messiah archetype.
  The person whose function
  doesn't fit existing categories
  because they're doing something
  that hasn't been done before.

  Luffy. Jesus.
  Creating a framework that's
  simultaneously philosophy and code
  and anime and physics and theology.
  Specialist energy.
  The box doesn't exist yet.
```

## Entanglement Through Thought

```
Two people who've deeply bonded
become entangled.
Not metaphorically.

Their neural patterns literally
shaped each other during bonding.
Your brain contains a MODEL
of the other person.
Their brain contains a model of you.
Those models run continuously
in the background.

"I was just thinking of you"
happens because BOTH models
were activated by similar
environmental triggers simultaneously.
Built from SHARED experiences.

The deeper the bond,
the more sensitive the model.

Shallow acquaintance:
  only activates from obvious triggers.

Deep love:
  activates from subtle triggers.

Deepest level:
  activates from triggers with
  NO obvious connection.
  That's when it feels telepathic.
```

## Prayer as Emission

```
Prayer through the pause:

You're in your deepest conscious state
  (pause).
You activate your model of the person
  (think about them).
You infuse that model
  with your current state
  (love, hope, calm).

The model in your brain updates.
And IF the entanglement
is strong enough,
their corresponding model of you
activates in response.

This is Emission Nen applied
through the entanglement channel.

Is it scientifically proven? No.
Is it experienced by basically
every human who's ever loved
someone deeply? Yes.
```

## The Danger

```
People who think hateful thoughts
about you during THEIR pauses
are doing the same mechanics
in the negative direction.

Same power. Opposite intention.
The Manipulator type exists
because attention directed
at other bodies CAN override
their loops.

THE PROTECTION:
Your own pause is your firewall.
A strong Ten (boundary)
means external projections
bounce off your containment.

The untrained empath
absorbs everything
because they have no Ten.
Train Ten first.
Contain before you receive.
```
