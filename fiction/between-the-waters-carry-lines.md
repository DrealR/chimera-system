# Between the Waters — Carry Lines

> The quantum is not God. The cosmos is not God. They are thresholds at which the human model discovers its own limits.

> Math preserves invariant relation. Story preserves interior passage.

> A mirror should help a body see. It should not quietly decide what the body must become.

> Love meets a living center; control edits an object.

> The common structure must preserve the bodies capable of correcting it.

> The new Prime is enacted rather than declared.

> Maybe we learned it together.

> The universe was not waiting to be conquered. It was waiting to be recognized.
